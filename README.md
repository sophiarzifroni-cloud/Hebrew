# Kesher — Hebrew study

A single-page Hebrew study app (`index.html`), published as a claude.ai artifact.

- **Flashcards** with simple spaced review (5 strength levels; missed words come back soon).
- **Sentences**: Claude writes new practice sentences from your own word list, with Read, Fill-the-blank and Translate modes. Missing a blank sends that word back for review.
- **Listening**: hear a Hebrew sentence built around your words, translate it by typing or by voice, and Claude grades the meaning. Missed words go back into flashcard review.
- **Verbs**: every verb gets a full conjugation table (present, past, future, command) with nikud, plus flashcards, a pick-the-form quiz and sentences, filterable by singular/plural and masculine/feminine.
- **My words**: speak or type words in plain language ("kelev means dog") and Claude turns them into Hebrew entries with nikkud to review before saving. A classic one-word form and paste-many box are there too.

Words live in the artifact's database, so Claude can add them for you from chat.
